PulseSensor.com article mockups

Animated CYD screens for real-time Playground data.

Three repo-ready visual directions: a raw PPG trust screen, a signal-quality warm-up screen, and a simple beat-by-beat tachogram.

1. Live PPG Signal

Default customer demo: raw waveform, beat markers, threshold, BPM, IBI, and amplitude.

Uses `getLatestSample()` every frame and `sawStartOfBeat()` for yellow detection markers.

2. Quality + BPM Warm-Up

Makes waiting legible: candidate beats become counted beats only after range and amplitude checks.

QUALITYSEARCHING
qualified 0/10quality 00/12

12-step quality meter locks at 10/12. Only qualified beats fill the 10-IBI BPM window.

3. Simple Tachogram

Each bar is one qualified inter-beat interval. The rhythm becomes visible without overexplaining HRV.

RMSSD 42msSDNN 51ms

Uses `getInterBeatIntervalMs()` after beat qualification. Keep this screen deliberately spare.
